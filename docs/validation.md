# Validation records

These notes record what has been built, tested and observed on the development Mac. They are not a guarantee of behavior on other systems.

## Build and tests

- `./build.sh` builds an optimized arm64 release bundle (debug info disabled), writes `Info.plist`, adds the app icon and ad-hoc signs the bundle. `Info.plist` lint and local code-signature verification pass.
- `./test.sh` runs the offline suites: appcast selection and filtering, Ed25519 archive signature verification with tamper and wrong-key rejection, installed-bundle discovery, JSON round trips, literal process arguments, large stdout/stderr capture, command deadlines, rejected updates, symlink-aware ZIP checks with fixture archives, DMG-over-ZIP preference, GitHub stable releases and asset architecture or ambiguity, signature gating, security report formatting, validated Homebrew tokens and custom app-folder arguments, Electron feeds and SHA-512, repository discovery, cask matching, vendor updaters, Homebrew actions, Trash-move validation, permissions, notifications, self-update feeds, proxy settings, a Keychain round trip under a throwaway service name, the presentation rules (dark default, status precedence, summary text, `~` paths), and for 0.4.0: ignore/skip/pre-release rules and their persistence, check schedules and migration, release-note sanitizing (scripts, styles, frames, handlers, `javascript:` and `http:` links, remote images, entities, size cap) and notes from Sparkle, Electron and GitHub feeds, pre-release selection, codesign output parsing and the approval store, history persistence (including older entries), scanner freshness after an in-place update and skipping of hidden and `*-backup-*` bundles, mas JSON and text parsing, matching, error wording and the Terminal script. DMG tests build fixture disk images with `hdiutil`: a good image (with a second app and an `Applications` link), wrong team (refused, app unchanged), the same image after approval (installed), several matching apps, a broken signature, a Gatekeeper rejection, version and checksum mismatches, a license agreement (refused until accepted), a junk file, a ZIP with a different team, and a failed post-install check (rolled back). After each case `hdiutil info` must no longer list the image, and nothing but the app may remain in its folder.
- No real app updates, Homebrew adoptions or cleanups, App Store installs, uninstalls, Trash moves or login registrations were performed during development.

## Launch checks

- 0.1.0: the app was launched in the logged-in session. A read-only check completed in about four seconds and reported 267 apps and packages with no available updates. Navigation, the console, the source filter, source overrides and the Snooze menu were exercised without changing any settings. A Sparkle app's detail showed its installed version, bundle ID, feed URL and "No compatible newer release found", with Update disabled.
- Launching the bundle with `open` from a restricted, non-GUI shell can fail with Launch Services error `-10827` and an abort during application registration. That is a limitation of that shell context, not of the app; the same bundle launches normally in the logged-in session.
- 0.2.0: a read-only smoke run found 80 apps (2 Electron feeds, 21 vendor-managed), checked both Electron feeds live (one newer release with a native arm64 ZIP was identified, not installed) and produced Homebrew cask suggestions for 19 apps. The bundle launched and quit.
- 0.3.0 and 0.3.1: every screen was rendered offscreen in dark and light with fake data (`--render-snapshots`) and reviewed. The bundle launched with its window at the 1040×680 minimum and quit cleanly.
- 0.3.2: the published Invoke Community Edition 1.8.5 arm64 ZIP (an Electron app; 456 entries, 14 framework links such as `Versions/Current -> A`) passed the new listing checks, was extracted into a temporary folder and passed the on-disk link checks; its SHA-512 matched the feed. `codesign --verify --deep --strict` and `spctl --assess` passed on the extracted app. Its signing team differs from the installed 1.8.1, so a real install would stop at the signing-team check. Nothing was installed.

- 0.4.0: the published Invoke Community Edition 1.8.5 arm64 DMG (SHA-512 matched the feed; no license agreement) was run through the full DMG path against a `ditto` copy of the installed 1.8.1 in a temporary folder. The install stopped with a signer change: the installed app is signed by the vendor's company team and the update by a different (individual) Developer ID team; both are Developer ID Application certificates and the update is notarized. After approving that team in a temporary approval store, the copy was replaced by 1.8.5 (this run used the earlier design, which kept a copy of 1.8.1; installs no longer keep one), `codesign --verify --deep --strict` and `spctl --assess` passed on the result, and the image was detached. The real app in `/Applications` was not modified and the temporary folder was removed.
- 0.4.0: the first build hung at launch (main thread at 100% CPU in a SwiftUI update loop driven by the menu bar item's visibility binding). After the fix the built app was launched and watched for over two minutes, including a full scan: CPU stayed near 0% when idle, and process samples showed the main thread waiting for events.
- 0.4.0: `mas` 7.0.0 was installed with Homebrew on the development Mac at the user's request. `mas outdated --json` (read-only) reported no pending App Store updates for the 26 App Store apps `mas list` found; App Store lookups were intermittent: about half of the runs, in any environment, ended after roughly 60 seconds with a lookup timeout, exit status 1 and no results. The app reports this as a temporary App Store connection problem, and the App Store items say the check could not finish, rather than claiming that mas is missing. No `mas update` was run.
- 0.4.0: the new screens were rendered offscreen in dark and light and reviewed. The menu bar status item itself can't be captured offscreen; its panel was rendered.
